## Momentum made visible

*Inside the app.*

Progress Tab turns the time you invest into meaningful activities into a visible, growing record of consistency. Instead of measuring raw hours as a chore, it reframes consistent effort as **momentum** — the accumulated force of small, repeated daily actions.

The app's dashboard makes that momentum visible: a momentum chart and an interactive progress view show how far you've already come, so that slow, day-to-day progress stops being invisible.

## Why it works

- **Track momentum, not time.** Logging hours is transactional and easy to abandon. Seeing momentum build is motivating and forward-looking.
- **Visible progress is easier to continue.** When progress is invisible, it feels like nothing is happening, and it's easy to quit. Making it visible changes that.
- **Consistency over intensity.** The goal is sustained, repeated effort over long periods — not doing more in less time.
- **Time as a universal unit.** Time applies fairly across almost any meaningful activity: reading, learning a language, exercising, building a side project, studying, or reflecting.
